Before you use OdontoAge

This is a research and educational tool. It re-implements published dental age-estimation methods so they can be inspected, tested, and cited. It does not make decisions about real people.

Estimated dental age is not chronological age and carries substantial uncertainty (often ±1 year or more in children, and several years in adults). It must never be the sole basis for a legal, forensic, immigration, or clinical decision.

Age assessment of living people — including asylum age-disputes — raises serious scientific and ethical concerns. Please follow the guidance of the relevant professional bodies and keep a qualified expert in the loop.

Ethics. Radiographic age estimation of living people (e.g. unaccompanied minors in asylum age-disputes) is scientifically limited and ethically contested — error margins are wide and a misclassified child can lose safeguarding. Several professional and radiology bodies have raised concerns about radiation exposure without clinical benefit. OdontoAge exists to make the published methods transparent and testable, not to endorse any particular use.
